# Game screens

Screens are pure functions of the latest snapshot (see `web-ui`). Colours and motion are in `design-system` and `motion`.

## Join and login pages (`/`, `/login`)

Both start with `HomeHero` (the animated `CowLogo` on a primary tile, the wordmark as the page `h1`, the one app slogan `home.tagline` on both pages; tapping the cow cycles through `home.moo.line1..4` in a `CowBubble` (`components/cow-bubble.tsx`; the cow's own antic lines show in a second, `aria-hidden` bubble, and only the tap lines are announced) outlined in `foreground`, since a `primary` outline disappears where it overlaps the primary tile; its tail is an inline SVG whose fill reaches 4 px up into the bubble to cover its bottom border, with the two sides stroked on top; an exact-fit tail left the border line across it on 1× and 1.25× screens) and `FloatingShapes` (five answer shapes in the candy palette behind the content). On phones the hero sits above the card; from `lg` the hero is left and the card right. The join card is a Play / Host switch (`Tabs` with the `bold` list; Play is the default, where QR links land, and holds the PIN flow; Host holds `LoginForm` from `features/auth/login-form.tsx`, shared with `/login`, and "Ask for one" when sign-up is open); each side has a screen-reader-only `h2`. The unchosen tab's text is `text-foreground/80`: the default 60 % fails 4.5:1 on the light track (axe in `a11y.e2e.ts`). `/login` keeps its own card (the `?redirect=` target). `AppHeader brand={false}` on these two routes (set in `__root.tsx`), so the logo is not shown twice. Keep the PIN boxes visible without scrolling on a 390 × 844 phone. On phones, focusing the PIN boxes scrolls the card to the top, using an absolutely positioned spacer below it that adds scroll room without moving the layout (the empty page fits the screen). It scrolls again on `visualViewport` resize, because the phone's keyboard opens afterwards and the browser scrolls the focused box to the top. `PinInput` moves between boxes with `focus({ preventScroll: true })`. A found game, including after a reload or a QR link, scrolls the wordmark to the top. Same-page `navigate` calls that only change the search (the `?pin=` sync) pass `resetScroll: false`; otherwise the router jumps back to the top and cancels the smooth scroll.

## Phone (`/play/$pin`, join page)

- `WaitingCow` (`components/waiting-cow.tsx`): a small cow with antics and bubbles on waiting screens: the top left corner of the lobby's `PlayerCard` (bubble to the right, clear of the avatar), next to the dots on "Answer sent!", and big next to the PIN in the projector lobby. Its bubble is absolutely positioned, so it never moves the layout; keep it from reaching past the screen's right edge.
- Phone layout is the default; the screen view is the exception. Touch targets at least 48 px high. Answer buttons fill the width and the lower half of the viewport.
- `/play/*` has no `AppHeader`: the root layout shows `GameBar` instead, one slim row with the question number, the `RoundChip` and the timer's ring during a question (the quiz title otherwise) and the `GameMenu` button at its right end, whose menu opens downwards; the timer's bar runs right under the row (`useTimer`, `TimerRing`, `TimerBar`). Phase screens do not repeat the question number or the timer; there is no bottom strip to keep free. Game pages are `min-h-dvh` (a `100%` chain follows the small viewport and left an empty strip on phones).
- `PausedNote` floats (`fixed`), never in the flow: a big pill centred over a dimmed phone screen, a pill near the top of the projector. The dim is z-40; the settings button and the host's message sit at z-50 so they stay bright and usable during a pause.
- Host messages and toasts are full width at the top and can be swiped away sideways: toasts through `swipeDirections` on the shared `Toaster`, the host's message through `SwipeAway` (`components/swipe-away.tsx`: pointer events, `touch-pan-y`, 80 px to go, the same element with or without a handler so swiped content stays off screen). The message (`AnnouncementBanner`) is `sticky` at the top of `/play/$pin`; with `onDismiss` it has a × too. Closed with × or by the host it wobbles out (`note-leaving`); swiped, it skips that and its space closes at once. While it leaves it is not clipped below, since its row shrinks under it. `useDismissable` (`features/play/dismissed-message.ts`, session storage per game) never hides an `important` message. A toast lying over the banner takes a swipe meant for the banner.
- Question pictures use `FitImage`: it measures its box (ResizeObserver) and sizes the picture to the box's width or height, whichever comes first, scaling small pictures up; CSS container units left it at 0 × 0. On the phone its box is `flex-[6_1_0px]` with `min-h-28` and `capHeight` (never taller than the picture at full width, so the rest goes to the answers' `flex-1` box). Use a `0px` basis, not `flex-6`: in a column without a set height a `0%` basis counts as the content's size and the picture's size feeds back into its box.
- Use `100dvh` based layouts, `viewport-fit=cover` safe-area padding, no horizontal scroll, no hover-only affordances.
- Forms: `inputMode="numeric"` for the PIN, `autoComplete="off"`, `enterKeyHint`.
- A persistent thin status bar shows connection state when it is not `connected`, using `errors.connectionLost`.
- Timer: a shrinking bar plus a ring with the seconds (`compact` on the phone's question screen), driven by `requestAnimationFrame` against the clock offset. Stop at 0, never negative; the last five seconds turn red and pulse.
- Lobby layouts: `features/play/lobby.tsx` only arranges; the parts (`PlayerCard`, `PinCard`, `GameInfoCard`) live in `lobby-parts.tsx` and the "About the game" lines in the pure, tested `lobby-info.ts`. Parts, `TeamCard` and `PickTeam` take no width of their own: `PhoneLobby` puts them in one `max-w-sm` column; `DesktopLobby` (from 64rem, chosen with `useMediaQuery`, not CSS breakpoints, so each layout stays readable on its own) uses two columns, the player and PIN sticky on the left, the team and the game on the right. A new phone screen that needs its own desktop version follows the same split.
- Lobby, top to bottom on phones: the player's card (avatar, "You're in", `TeamBanner` in `bg-primary` with "Your team" and the team's name, or "You're playing on your own"; then waiting for the host and the player count), the PIN card, the team card (members with their count, the answer mode, the captain's choices), "About the game". The team's name is only on the banner.
- Lobby card and `GameMenu` show the game PIN (the menu in every phase, with the quiz title, then the player's avatar, name and team, and "Leave game" with a `ConfirmDialog` until the game is finished; leaving forgets the token, toasts and goes home). Its rows are touch-sized (`touchItem` / `touchRadio`: `min-h-12`, `text-base`), unlike the host's compact dropdowns.
- Teammates offline (team card, `TeamPicksPanel`): a dashed chip or tag with "offline" / "left" and a faded avatar (`opacity-40 grayscale` on the avatar only, so the text keeps its contrast); never "thinking…". The picks panel's open state is `usePicksOpen(pin, default)` (`features/play/picks-open.ts`, session storage per game).
- `ConfirmDialog` buttons stretch with `sm:flex-1` only: `flex-1` in the phone's column footer squashes them to half height. The lobby's PIN is big with "Copy PIN" (`copyText` in `lib/clipboard.ts`: the Clipboard API, else the copy command, since phones on the venue wifi often use plain http where `navigator.clipboard` is missing), and an "About the game" card lists the question count, how players score and only the rules that are on, in player words (`play.info.*`), not the host's setting labels.
- Never a bare "Answer sent!": from the answer until the next question the player sees what they sent. `MyAnswer` (it reads `question ?? reveal.question`) is on the answered view (answering alone, and a team member's pick or vote until the team is done; captain decides waits on the question view until the confirmation), on the held-back reveal (`AnswerKept`) and on the normal reveal unless the answer is correct (the correct answer shows it already); a team of more than one gets `TeamSummary` with the picks once done.
- Answered view: a check, "Answer sent!" and the count. A player answering alone (no teammates) also gets `MyAnswer` (`features/play/my-answer.tsx`): the answer as chips in its option colours and symbols (true first, false second, as the buttons), an ordered list, or the text or number, plus "Answered in … s" (`PlayerSnapshot.myAnswerTimeMs`) and "Question n of m"; never whether it is right. Teams of more than one get `TeamSummary` (see `team-modes`). With `answerChanges` it adds "Change answer" and the seconds left (`changeWindow` in `features/play/change-window.ts`, server time; frozen and disabled while paused). Changing shows the question again with the previous answer as `initial` on every question input, plus "Keep it"; it falls back to the answered view when the lock-in starts or the change is acked.
- Join: the PIN comes first, the name and avatar only after the PIN is accepted. A scheduled game shows "Starts …" with "Add to calendar" and "Leave" above the form until its time (server clock from the public info's `serverNow`; the info is fetched again every 30 s while waiting): the details can be filled in ahead, Join stays locked, and the PIN (address) and details (`getDraft`/`storeDraft` in `player-storage.ts`) survive a reload. Keep `?pin=` a number in route search (the router quotes a string of digits). `PinInput` (one box per digit) and the lazy `QrScanner` (camera + `jsqr`, decoded on the device; offered only when `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` exists, i.e. HTTPS or localhost). `pinFromScan` in `lib/pin.ts` accepts the join link, a play link or the bare digits.
- End-of-game review (`podium.tsx`), with "Your team's answer" and the fallback note for team answers (`PlayerQuestionResult.teamAnswer`, `fallback`): one `<details>` card per question, open by default, the question as a heading alone on the first row, level result chips, the card tinted with its result tone (`--tone`) through sRGB `color-mix` (see `design-system`).

## Projector (`/screen/$pin`)

The question phase gets a fixed height (`h-[calc(100dvh-5.5rem)]`, header plus the page's bottom padding) and always fits: one top row (question number, round, answered count, timer ring) with the bar under it, the question, then a picture (`FitImage`, `flex-[3_1_0px]`, `capHeight`) above the answers' grid (`flex-[2_1_0px]`). Targets 1080p at 2 to 4 m viewing distance: question text at 40 px or more, options at 32 px or more, the QR code at least 300 px in the lobby, the join URL spelled out next to it. Shows answer count live, the distribution bar chart in reveal (`RevealLayout`, shared with the results summary: question and image left, bars right, figures below), top 10 in scoreboard, top 3 podium at the end (`PodiumStage`, also used on phones in its compact form). If the screen is also the host (same browser session), Space or Right arrow triggers "next" and the same action floats as a button bottom right; otherwise the screen is passive. The projector keeps its header. A game with branding (`snapshot.branding`, copied at creation) shows `BrandingMark` above the quiz title in the lobby and above "Game over" on the podium; phone snapshots carry it only when the host chose "Also on the phones", and the phone lobby shows it above the player's card.

## Host control (`/host/games/$pin`)

The lobby phase has its own layout, `HostLobby` (`features/host/host-lobby.tsx`): a join card (quiz title, settings line, online and latency badges, the QR code next to the PIN when the card is at least `@lg` wide, the join link, Copy link where the clipboard exists, Open projector), a scheduled game's `ScheduledCard` (opening time, Open now, Change time, Call off) until `useTimeReached(scheduledAt)` flips and, beside it, Start (only once someone has joined), `LobbyPlayers` (rows grouped by `lobbyGroups`, no scores: classic rows `minmax(13rem)` with the name on up to two lines, two to a laptop column, since three cut 24-character names mid-word; team rows draggable; offline rows dashed with a faded avatar so the text keeps its contrast; a remove button per row) and the message box, with End game last. The review panel is not shown in the lobby. `MessageBox` (lobby and running game) lists the game's quick messages (`snapshot.quickMessages`, built-in ones translated through `messageText`) with their time; the time row starts on "own time" (each message's own, free text until cleared) and has "custom" (a seconds field). "Save" writes to the library over HTTP, then sends `addQuickMessage`; "Edit" turns the chips into rows with a `DurationField` (`setQuickMessageTime`) and a remove button, both for this game only. Player toasts (join, offline, back, left, removed) come from `useHostToasts` (`features/host/host-toasts.tsx`): the pure `playerEvents` / `playerToasts` diff consecutive host snapshots and batch for 1.5 s into one toast per kind with a fixed id; the bell `HostToastsToggle` beside `RoundTripBadge` mutes them per browser. Only the host control mounts it, never the projector. The e2e specs wait for the `connectedOfTotal` badge ("1 / 1 connected"), so keep it in the lobby.


After the lobby the page is the live board (phase 43). From `lg` it fills the window below the app header (`lg:h-[calc(100dvh-5.5rem)]`) and never scrolls; the host sidebar is hidden (`routes/host/route.tsx`, also while the snapshot is loading; the lobby keeps it). Pieces:
- **Top bar** (in the route file): back to the quizzes, quiz title, PIN, phase and question, connection badges, Lock screen and Open screen (icons only below `xl`) and `EndGameButton`.
- **`QuestionStrip`** (`features/host/question-strip.tsx`): the round's intro, or the question with its badges, the counts per option as chips with the correct ones marked (free answers: the correct answer and the most common answers), and between questions "On the screens: …" and the figures (`dl`: average time, fastest right). The timer ring sits in the strip's corner and the bar along its bottom edge.
- **`PlayerBoard`** (`features/host/player-board.tsx`, model in `board.ts`): one tile per player, grouped per team in team mode (the header has the team's one answer, who set it, "Confirmed" / "Suggesting (2 of 3)" / "Done" while it runs and "(Majority, not confirmed)" afterwards).
  - A tile shows waiting (dashed, hourglass), the answer (`AnswerChip`: option colour and symbol), the time and the live verdict: the server fills `currentAnswers[].correct` while a question runs.
  - Between questions the tile turns green or red, with the points and the rank arrow in place of the time.
  - Sizes: `fitTileSize` picks the largest of lg/md/sm that fits the measured scroll box (`tileMetrics` must match the tile markup), and a layout effect steps one size down if it still overflows. sm tiles are one line (time and points in the tooltip), so 60 players fit 1280 × 800.
  - "Hide answers" and the View menu (tiles/list, sort by name, rank, answer time or missing first, side panels) are per-device options in `board-options.ts` (`quizmoo.hostBoard`, listed in the storage inventory); the menu stays open while the host changes them.
- **Right rail:** `Controls`, then `GamePanels` (`features/host/game-panels.tsx`): tabs Standings, Message (dot while a message shows), Players (`TeamModesPanel` + `PlayerPanel`: remove, captain, move), Questions (`ReviewPanel`, "Show again") and Preview. Only the open tab is mounted. The Message panel and its field share the name "Message": e2e specs target the field as `getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Message' })`.
- **Host menu:** the top bar's menu button (laptops only) sets `sidebar` in the board options. On the game page `routes/host/route.tsx` keeps `HostNav` mounted in a wrapper whose width springs like the editor's settings column (`duration-700 ease-spring-soft`, `overflow-x-clip`, `invisible` and `inert` when closed). `data-sidebar-closed` keeps the toasts from centring on a closed sidebar (`styles.css`).
- **Phase replacements:** grading puts `GradingPanel` in the board's place, and a finished game shows the podium section there.

Below `lg` the same pieces form one column and `Controls` sticks to the bottom.

`Controls` (`features/host/controls.tsx`, placement `rail` or `bottom`) is rendered once: at the end of the page's flex column on phones (`sticky` to the bottom; the outer container is a flex column below `lg`, because a sticky grid item is confined to its own grid area) and at the top of the rail on laptops (`useMediaQuery` picks the place). It holds the primary action, the alternative and the question's Pause, +30 s and Skip (one row on phones, icons from `sm`), plus the phase hints; `EndGameButton` is in the top bar. `Standings` (`features/host/standings.tsx`) lists teams and players by rank with the real scores (also while answers wait for the end, with a note that only the host sees them), the first 10 with "Show all", and "Results so far" (the results page in a new tab) until the game is finished. Names on the host control wrap (`wrap-break-word`), never `truncate`: names are up to 24 characters. A projector opened from the host's browser attaches as the host and gets the full snapshot, so `ScreenScoreboard` drops rank arrows and round points itself while `answersHidden`.

The bar starts with the chip of what everyone sees (`screenStatus` in `features/host/screen-status.ts`, pure and tested: one line when phones and projector show the same, two once the final results are released to one of them). Between questions it offers "Show answers" (`ShowAnswersDialog`: last question, all so far, or a checklist of the closed questions, sent as `showAnswers`) and, while the chosen questions are on the screens, Previous and Next (`showQuestion` within `reviewQueue`). Phones and projector show such a question's reveal also while answers are held back (`answersHidden && !reviewing` is the hidden view), without points, score or rank lines. `ScreenPreview` (`features/host/screen-preview.tsx`, the Preview tab, opening on the Phone tab) shows the projector live in an inert, scaled iframe (loaded only while open; the CSP allows `frame-ancestors 'self'` for it) and, on the Phone tab, `PhoneScreenPreview`: the screen the host has sent to the phones, drawn from the host snapshot in a phone frame (question and answers, answer saved, the correct answer with a note that each phone adds its own result, the standings, game over), not one player's live phone (the user asked for that, October 2026). "Lock screen" (header button) sends `lock` at once when the snapshot has `hasLockCode`, else `LockDialog` offers a code for this game; the server then locks every game of the host. While `me.screenLocked` is true the host layout (`routes/host/route.tsx`) renders `LockScreen` (`features/host/lock-screen.tsx`: lock code or "Use my password", `POST /api/me/unlock`, Log out) instead of every host page; the game page renders nothing of a snapshot with `locked`. The browser notices through a 423 from any request (the query and mutation caches invalidate `me`), the `quizmoo-screen-lock` BroadcastChannel between tabs (`lib/screen-lock.ts`), `me` refetched on window focus, and the snapshot's `locked` flag. The projector opened by the host drives nothing while locked. "End game" opens a dialog: "Show the podium" (`end`) or "Close the game" (`close`, then the results page); in the lobby it is a confirmation and `close`. E2E specs end their games through `e2e/close-game.ts` or click "Show the podium".

## Round intros (phase 40)

Each audience has its own `round-intro.tsx` (`features/play`, `features/screen`, `features/host`): "Round 2 of 5" (`play.roundOf`), the title as a heading, the info, the picture, the question count and a countdown bar when the intro is timed (frozen while `pausedAt` is set). The phone's intro fits 390 × 844 without scrolling. Question, answered and reveal screens show the round as a chip (`snapshot.group`), and the lobbies say "24 questions in 4 rounds" when `groupCount > 0`. The host control's intro view has "Start round" and Hold / Release; at a round's end the main button follows `primaryAction` (the round's answers, its standings, then "Next round").

## Results page (`/host/results/$gameId`)

`ResultsHero` (state, mode, date, length, the PIN, figures, and the actions: CSV, projector, Delete), then `WinnerCard` (winners on a tie, the team's best player, second and third) beside `QuestionHighlights` (easiest and hardest), all from the pure, tested `features/results/summary.ts`; then bold tabs: Players, Teams (team mode) and Questions (split into rounds by `questionRuns`). Delete navigates away before dropping the results query, or the still-mounted query fetches the deleted game (a 404 the e2e specs fail on).

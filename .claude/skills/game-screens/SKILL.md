# Game screens

Screens are pure functions of the latest snapshot (see `web-ui`). Colours and motion are in `design-system` and `motion`.

## Join and login pages (`/`, `/login`)

Both start with `HomeHero` (the animated `CowLogo` on a primary tile, the wordmark as the page `h1`, the one app slogan `home.tagline` on both pages; tapping the cow cycles through `home.moo.line1..4` in a speech bubble outlined in `foreground`, since a `primary` outline disappears where it overlaps the primary tile; its tail is an inline SVG whose fill reaches 4 px up into the bubble to cover its bottom border, with the two sides stroked on top; an exact-fit tail left the border line across it on 1× and 1.25× screens) and `FloatingShapes` (five answer shapes in the candy palette behind the content). On phones the hero sits above the card; from `lg` the hero is left and the card right. The card heading is an `h2`. `AppHeader brand={false}` on these two routes (set in `__root.tsx`), so the logo is not shown twice. Keep the PIN boxes visible without scrolling on a 390 × 844 phone. On phones, focusing the PIN boxes scrolls the card to the top, using an absolutely positioned spacer below it that adds scroll room without moving the layout (the empty page fits the screen). It scrolls again on `visualViewport` resize, because the phone's keyboard opens afterwards and the browser scrolls the focused box to the top. `PinInput` moves between boxes with `focus({ preventScroll: true })`. A found game, including after a reload or a QR link, scrolls the wordmark to the top. Same-page `navigate` calls that only change the search (the `?pin=` sync) pass `resetScroll: false`; otherwise the router jumps back to the top and cancels the smooth scroll.

## Phone (`/play/$pin`, join page)

- Phone layout is the default; the screen view is the exception. Touch targets at least 48 px high. Answer buttons fill the width and the lower half of the viewport.
- `/play/*` has no `AppHeader`: the root layout shows `GameMenu` instead, a fixed 48 px round button in the bottom left corner (thumb reach, and away from the messages and toasts at the top) whose menu opens upwards. The root layout gives game pages `GAME_MENU_SPACE` bottom padding, so the end of every page scrolls clear of it; nothing needs to keep a corner free.
- Host messages and toasts are full width at the top and can be swiped away sideways: toasts through `swipeDirections` on the shared `Toaster`, the host's message through `SwipeAway` (`components/swipe-away.tsx`: pointer events, `touch-pan-y`, 80 px to go, the same element with or without a handler so swiped content stays off screen). The message (`AnnouncementBanner`) is `sticky` at the top of `/play/$pin`; with `onDismiss` it has a × too. Closed with × or by the host it wobbles out (`note-leaving`); swiped, it skips that and its space closes at once. While it leaves it is not clipped below, since its row shrinks under it. `useDismissable` (`features/play/dismissed-message.ts`, session storage per game) never hides an `important` message. A toast lying over the banner takes a swipe meant for the banner.
- A question image on the phone sits in a `flex-3` box (absolutely positioned `img`, `max-h-full`, `object-contain`, `min-h-28`) next to the answers' `flex-2` box, so image and answers fit the screen together (a 390 × 844 phone with four options shows everything without scrolling).
- Use `100dvh` based layouts, `viewport-fit=cover` safe-area padding, no horizontal scroll, no hover-only affordances.
- Forms: `inputMode="numeric"` for the PIN, `autoComplete="off"`, `enterKeyHint`.
- A persistent thin status bar shows connection state when it is not `connected`, using `errors.connectionLost`.
- Timer: a shrinking bar plus a ring with the seconds, driven by `requestAnimationFrame` against the clock offset. Stop at 0, never negative; the last five seconds turn red and pulse.
- Lobby layouts: `features/play/lobby.tsx` only arranges; the parts (`PlayerCard`, `PinCard`, `GameInfoCard`) live in `lobby-parts.tsx` and the "About the game" lines in the pure, tested `lobby-info.ts`. Parts, `TeamCard` and `PickTeam` take no width of their own: `PhoneLobby` puts them in one `max-w-sm` column; `DesktopLobby` (from 64rem, chosen with `useMediaQuery`, not CSS breakpoints, so each layout stays readable on its own) uses two columns, the player and PIN sticky on the left, the team and the game on the right. A new phone screen that needs its own desktop version follows the same split.
- Lobby, top to bottom on phones: the player's card (avatar, "You're in", `TeamBanner` in `bg-primary` with "Your team" and the team's name, or "You're playing on your own"; then waiting for the host and the player count), the PIN card, the team card (members with their count, the answer mode, the captain's choices), "About the game". The team's name is only on the banner.
- Lobby card and `GameMenu` show the game PIN (the menu in every phase, with the quiz title, then the player's avatar, name and team, and "Leave game" with a `ConfirmDialog` until the game is finished; leaving forgets the token, toasts and goes home). Its rows are touch-sized (`touchItem` / `touchRadio`: `min-h-12`, `text-base`), unlike the host's compact dropdowns.
- Teammates offline (team card, `TeamPicksPanel`): a dashed chip or tag with "offline" / "left" and a faded avatar (`opacity-40 grayscale` on the avatar only, so the text keeps its contrast); never "thinking…". The picks panel's open state is `usePicksOpen(pin, default)` (`features/play/picks-open.ts`, session storage per game).
- `ConfirmDialog` buttons stretch with `sm:flex-1` only: `flex-1` in the phone's column footer squashes them to half height. The lobby's PIN is big with "Copy PIN" (`copyText` in `lib/clipboard.ts`: the Clipboard API, else the copy command, since phones on the venue wifi often use plain http where `navigator.clipboard` is missing), and an "About the game" card lists the question count, how players score and only the rules that are on, in player words (`play.info.*`), not the host's setting labels.
- Never a bare "Answer sent!": from the answer until the next question the player sees what they sent. `MyAnswer` (it reads `question ?? reveal.question`) is on the answered view (answering alone, and a team member's pick or vote until the team is done; captain decides waits on the question view until the confirmation), on the held-back reveal (`AnswerKept`) and on the normal reveal unless the answer is correct (the correct answer shows it already); a team of more than one gets `TeamSummary` with the picks once done.
- Answered view: a check, "Answer sent!" and the count. A player answering alone (no teammates) also gets `MyAnswer` (`features/play/my-answer.tsx`): the answer as chips in its option colours and symbols (true first, false second, as the buttons), an ordered list, or the text or number, plus "Answered in … s" (`PlayerSnapshot.myAnswerTimeMs`) and "Question n of m"; never whether it is right. Teams of more than one get `TeamSummary` (see `team-modes`). With `answerChanges` it adds "Change answer" and the seconds left (`changeWindow` in `features/play/change-window.ts`, server time; frozen and disabled while paused). Changing shows the question again with the previous answer as `initial` on every question input, plus "Keep it"; it falls back to the answered view when the lock-in starts or the change is acked.
- Join: the PIN comes first, the name and avatar only after the PIN is accepted. A scheduled game shows "Starts …" with "Add to calendar" and "Leave" above the form until its time (server clock from the public info's `serverNow`; the info is fetched again every 30 s while waiting): the details can be filled in ahead, Join stays locked, and the PIN (address) and details (`getDraft`/`storeDraft` in `player-storage.ts`) survive a reload. Keep `?pin=` a number in route search (the router quotes a string of digits). `PinInput` (one box per digit) and the lazy `QrScanner` (camera + `jsqr`, decoded on the device; offered only when `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` exists, i.e. HTTPS or localhost). `pinFromScan` in `lib/pin.ts` accepts the join link, a play link or the bare digits.
- End-of-game review (`podium.tsx`), with "Your team's answer" and the fallback note for team answers (`PlayerQuestionResult.teamAnswer`, `fallback`): one `<details>` card per question, open by default, the question as a heading alone on the first row, level result chips, the card tinted with its result tone (`--tone`) through sRGB `color-mix` (see `design-system`).

## Projector (`/screen/$pin`)

Targets 1080p at 2 to 4 m viewing distance: question text at 40 px or more, options at 32 px or more, the QR code at least 300 px in the lobby, the join URL spelled out next to it. Shows answer count live, the distribution bar chart in reveal (`RevealLayout`, shared with the results summary: question and image left, bars right, figures below), top 10 in scoreboard, top 3 podium at the end (`PodiumStage`, also used on phones in its compact form). If the screen is also the host (same browser session), Space or Right arrow triggers "next" and the same action floats as a button bottom right; otherwise the screen is passive. The projector keeps its header. A game with branding (`snapshot.branding`, copied at creation) shows `BrandingMark` above the quiz title in the lobby and above "Game over" on the podium; phone snapshots carry it only when the host chose "Also on the phones", and the phone lobby shows it above the player's card.

## Host control (`/host/games/$pin`)

The lobby phase has its own layout, `HostLobby` (`features/host/host-lobby.tsx`): a join card (quiz title, settings line, online and latency badges, the QR code next to the PIN when the card is at least `@lg` wide, the join link, Copy link where the clipboard exists, Open projector), a scheduled game's `ScheduledCard` (opening time, Open now, Change time, Call off) until `useTimeReached(scheduledAt)` flips and, beside it, Start (only once someone has joined), `LobbyPlayers` (rows grouped by `lobbyGroups`, no scores: classic rows `minmax(13rem)` with the name on up to two lines, two to a laptop column, since three cut 24-character names mid-word; team rows draggable; offline rows dashed with a faded avatar so the text keeps its contrast; a remove button per row) and the message box, with End game last. The review panel is not shown in the lobby. `MessageBox` (lobby and running game) lists the game's quick messages (`snapshot.quickMessages`, built-in ones translated through `messageText`) with their time; the time row starts on "own time" (each message's own, free text until cleared) and has "custom" (a seconds field). "Save" writes to the library over HTTP, then sends `addQuickMessage`; "Edit" turns the chips into rows with a `DurationField` (`setQuickMessageTime`) and a remove button, both for this game only. Player toasts (join, offline, back, left, removed) come from `useHostToasts` (`features/host/host-toasts.tsx`): the pure `playerEvents` / `playerToasts` diff consecutive host snapshots and batch for 1.5 s into one toast per kind with a fixed id; the bell `HostToastsToggle` beside `RoundTripBadge` mutes them per browser. Only the host control mounts it, never the projector. The e2e specs wait for the `connectedOfTotal` badge ("1 / 1 connected"), so keep it in the lobby.


During a running question `LiveQuestion` shows the answers coming in. In reveal, scoreboard and when a question is shown again, `ShownQuestion` (`features/host/shown-question.tsx`) says what the screens show and gives the question's type, time, points, image, correct answer, answered and right counts, average time, the fastest right answer (a row of its own: it holds a whole name), the bars and who did not answer.

After the lobby, `Controls` (`features/host/controls.tsx`) is a bar that stays in view: rendered at the end of the page's flex column on phones (`sticky` above the tab bar; the outer container is a flex column below `lg`, because a sticky grid item is confined to its own grid area) and at the top of the main column on laptops (`useMediaQuery` picks the place; render it once). It holds the primary action, the alternative and the question's Pause, +30 s and Skip (one row on phones, icons from `sm`), plus the phase hints; `EndGameButton` sits below the question instead. `Standings` (`features/host/standings.tsx`) lists teams and players by rank with the real scores (also while answers wait for the end, with a note that only the host sees them), the first 10 with "Show all", and "Results so far" (the results page in a new tab) until the game is finished. Names on the host control wrap (`wrap-break-word`), never `truncate`: names are up to 24 characters. A projector opened from the host's browser attaches as the host and gets the full snapshot, so `ScreenScoreboard` drops rank arrows and round points itself while `answersHidden`.

## Results page (`/host/results/$gameId`)

`ResultsHero` (state, mode, date, length, the PIN, figures, and the actions: CSV, projector, Delete), then `WinnerCard` (winners on a tie, the team's best player, second and third) beside `QuestionHighlights` (easiest and hardest), all from the pure, tested `features/results/summary.ts`; then bold tabs: Players, Teams (team mode) and Questions. Delete navigates away before dropping the results query, or the still-mounted query fetches the deleted game (a 404 the e2e specs fail on).
